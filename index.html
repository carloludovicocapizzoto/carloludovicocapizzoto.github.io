About

I was born in Messina, I'm 21, and I love growing things from zero: events, communities, products.

I hold a bachelor's degree in CLEACC from Bocconi University (Economics and Management for Arts, Culture and Communication), with a thesis on brand communication in luxury hospitality. Since September 2026 I've been enrolled in the MSc in Digital Marketing & Data Analytics at Emlyon Business School in Paris.

I've played trumpet and piano for over ten years: art is the starting point of almost everything I do. Along the way I worked in Sales & Marketing at Villa d'Este and was Project Manager of a 250+ participant event with B.lab Bocconi.

I co-founded GrowMi, which promotes emerging artists through live events in Milan. I lead a team of 12, handle strategy, partnerships and social media, and I built our website and ticket-screening software using AI.

I'm looking for an internship or role starting October 2026 in digital marketing, brand communication or event management, where creativity meets data.
