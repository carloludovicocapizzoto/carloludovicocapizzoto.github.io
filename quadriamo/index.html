How it works

Set it up once. Then it's four things a row.

1

Pick your country

Your currency is set for you and every amount follows it. Then check the rule, your payday and your categories.

2

Log in seconds

Date, description, category, amount. The bucket and the type fill in on their own; recurring bills appear by themselves.

3

See what's left

Every bucket shows what came in, what went out and what remains. Leftovers roll into next month; the checks flag mistakes.

Built for real life

One optional column keeps the rule honest when plans change

Paid from another bucket

Big grocery run paid with your Quality of Life money? Pick that bucket in Paid from. Groceries still count as groceries; the money leaves the right bucket.

Extra income, one destination

A bonus or a freelance payment can go entirely to one bucket, like Investments. Leave it blank and it's split 55-20-15-10.

FAQ

Questions

Which programs does it work in?

Excel 2016 or newer on Windows and Mac, and Excel on the web. Pro also comes as a Google Sheets version you copy into your Drive with one click.

Are there macros?

No. Everything is formulas, so there are no security warnings and nothing to enable.

Can I change the 55-20-15-10 rule?

Yes. Percentages, bucket names, categories and the day your month starts are all in Settings.

Do live prices and exchange rates always update?

They update by themselves in Google Sheets and in Excel for Microsoft 365. In older Excel versions you type the rate or price once in the manual column.

Is this financial advice?

No. Quadriamo is a budgeting tool. Investment projections use a fixed expected return and are not a promise of future results.
